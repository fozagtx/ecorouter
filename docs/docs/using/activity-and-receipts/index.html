Using EcoRouter

Receipts

Every payment attempt leaves a receipt in Activity.

The Activity screen lists every payment attempt as a receipt. A receipt records who paid (the spender), the service id, the amount, the memo, the time, and the outcome.

Outcomes

OutcomeMeaningMoney moved
SettledEvery check passed and USDC went to the service's payee.Yes
HeldA limit caught the payment. It may become an approval request.No
DeclinedThe owner declined an approval request.No
RefusedA check that cannot be approved failed, or an agent hit a limit.No

Held, declined and refused receipts carry the rule that stopped the payment and, for limits, how far over it was.

On chain

On Stellar testnet, a settled payment emits a pay event from the team-account contract carrying the spender, service, payee, amount, memo and the spender's spend this period. Settled exceptions emit a request settled event. The receipt in the app links to the Stellar transaction so anyone can check it. The replay key passed to pay means the same payment cannot be settled twice, even if the app retries.

A payment that fails a check never reaches the chain as a transfer, so held, declined and refused receipts are kept by the app; requests and decisions are also recorded as contract events.

The app reads recent contract events from Soroban RPC as far back as the node keeps them—up to about seven days; the public testnet node has kept as little as about a day. A refusal detected before submission is kept only in the current browser and has no transaction link.

Approvals

Over a limit, a payment is held, not failed, and the owner decides.

Assistant

Ask for a payment in plain words. Five stages check it, and any one can refuse.

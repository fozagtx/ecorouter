Agent limits

What an agent key can do, what the policy limits, and what remains outside the contract.

An agent signs only as its own spender address. pay does not accept a recipient address; it resolves a service id through the registry. The policy limits the allowance, period, per-payment amount, payment count, expiry and approved services. The owner can pause or remove the spender.

Agents cannot file approval requests. A refused agent payment ends at the contract check that failed. To permit a different payment, the owner must change the policy.

The SDK worker runs one agent and one service per Render worker. Its key is a hot key held by the worker environment; the policy does not prevent someone with that key from spending within its limits. Keep the policy narrow and rotate or remove the spender if the key is exposed.

EcoRouter is testnet-only and unaudited. The registered testnet services pay demo addresses controlled by the deployer; they are not outside merchants.

Sources: contracts/team-account/src/lib.rs (pay, request_approval, set_policy, set_spender_paused, remove_spender); packages/sdk/src/worker.ts; deployments/testnet.json.

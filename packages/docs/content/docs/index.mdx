title: Introduction
description: EcoRouter is a shared USDC spending account on Stellar for teams and their AI agents.
---

EcoRouter gives a team one shared USDC account on Stellar. Every spender on that account, whether a person or an AI agent, pays under its own on-chain policy: an allowance per period, a cap per payment, a maximum number of payments, an expiry date and a list of approved services. The policy is enforced by a Soroban contract that holds the money.

> **The agent proposes. Only the contract authorizes.**

An assistant or an agent can suggest a payment, and the app can pre-check it, but neither can move money on its own. The only thing that moves money is the `team-account` contract, and it runs the same checks on every payment, in the same order.

<Callout type="warn" title="Honest status">
EcoRouter runs on **Stellar testnet only** and the contracts are **not audited**. Use test funds. The sandbox runs the same rules in your browser, using a TypeScript mirror of the contract, so you can try everything without a wallet.
</Callout>

## What a team does with it

1. **Fund one account.** The owner deposits USDC into the team account contract.
2. **Add spenders.** Each person or agent gets a policy. The owner can pause or remove a spender at any time.
3. **Pay registered services.** Payments go to services in the on-chain [service registry](/concepts/services), named by id, never by address.
4. **Hold what breaks a rule.** A payment over a limit is held, not failed. Nothing moves until the owner approves and the payment is settled in a separate step.
5. **Keep a receipt for everything.** Settled, held, declined and refused payments all show up in Activity, with a link to the Stellar transaction when one exists.

---
title: Spenders and policies
description: Every person and every agent pays under its own on-chain policy.
---

A **spender** is a Stellar address that may pay from the team account: a teammate's wallet or an AI agent's key. Each spender has exactly one **policy**, set by the owner.

## What a policy holds

| Rule | Field | Example | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kind | `kind` | Person or Agent | Agents cannot ask for approvals. |
| Label | `label` | "Sam" | A display name. |
| Allowance per period | `allowance` | $1,000 | Total the spender may pay in one period. |
| Period | `period` | 30 days | Length of a period. Periods are fixed windows counted from when the spender was added. |
| Cap per payment | `max_payment` | $250 | No single payment may be larger. Must not exceed the allowance. |
| Max payments per period | `max_payments` | 20 | How many payments the spender may make in one period. |
| Expiry | `expires_at` | 2026-12-31 | After this time, the policy stops authorizing anything. |
| Approved services | `services` | `inference-credits`, `gpu-rental` | The only service ids this spender may pay (up to 32). |
| Paused | `paused` | false | When true, the spender cannot pay at all. |

Every limit must be positive. A zero never means "no limit".

A policy only ever narrows what a spender can do. There is no rule that lets a spender pay an address directly, and no rule that lets one spender use another's allowance.

## The arithmetic

The app shows the same arithmetic the contract runs. For a spender with an allowance of $900, $340 already spent and a cap of $250:

```text
allowance left  = $900 − $340      = $560
pay $120        → $120 ≤ cap $250  ✓
                → $120 ≤ $560 left ✓   → settles, $440 left
pay $320        → $320 > cap $250  ✗   → held, over cap by $70
```

## Managing spenders

- **Add** a spender with an address and a policy.
- **Edit** the policy at any time. Changes apply to the next payment; the current period and its usage are kept.
- **Pause** a spender to stop them immediately without losing their history. **Resume** to undo.
- **Remove** a spender to delete their policy. Their address can no longer pay; past receipts stay.

## People and agents

Both are spenders and both go through the same checks. The difference is what happens after a payment breaks a limit: a person's payment is held and can become an [approval request](/concepts/approvals); an [agent](/concepts/agents)'s payment is refused and stops there. The contract knows which is which from the policy's `kind`.
